Bundled Mushaf — Hafs, QCF V4, 604 pages

Calligraphy, font outlines and original Tajweed palettes:
King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex (KFGQPC).
The original fonts are provided by Quran Foundation:
https://verses.quran.foundation/fonts/quran/hafs/v4/colrv1/ttf/p{page}.ttf
https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/fonts/font-rendering/
https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/

The application stores losslessly compressed copies. Decoding restores each
original TTF byte for byte, checked against its pinned SHA-256. No glyph, color,
outline, font table or scripture is edited. The files are integrated application
assets and are not offered as a separate download or API resource.

Page/line and word metadata: Quranic Universal Library, Tarteel AI (QUL).
https://qul.tarteel.ai/resources/mushaf-layout/19
Mechanical CSV export and font-to-word mapping: hussainsajib/qpc-v4-mushaf-bundle,
revision f84ccb5688d5404748a0d38e6c53e09df4469ec9.
https://github.com/hussainsajib/qpc-v4-mushaf-bundle/blob/f84ccb5688d5404748a0d38e6c53e09df4469ec9/LICENSE-NOTICE.md
The default page's glyphs and layout were checked against the application's
existing Quran Foundation page fixtures. Text/word data remains attributed to
its upstream authors. No font or religious text is generated by the application.

Alternative Unicode reading fonts are already bundled with their own licenses
in assets/fonts. They share the fixed line assignments; the original per-letter
Tajweed palette belongs to QCF V4 and is displayed with the original page font.
